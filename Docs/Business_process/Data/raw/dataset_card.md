## 2. Raw files (stored unchanged in `data/raw/`)

The original ZIP and the 11 extracted `.txt` files are kept exactly as downloaded. Nothing in `data/raw/` is edited; all cleaning happens in Phase IV.

Row counts were produced by `quality_check_lite.py` and exclude the header line.

| File | Rows | Columns | Size | One record represents |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| agency.txt | 3 | 4 | 1 KB | One transport operator (company) |
| calendar.txt | 2 | 10 | 1 KB | One service pattern: which weekdays run, and the start and end dates |
| calendar_dates.txt | 0 | 3 | 1 KB | One exception to a service pattern (no rows in this feed) |
| fare_attributes.txt | 49 | 6 | 1 KB | One fare: price, currency, payment method, transfers |
| fare_rules.txt | 62 | 2 | 1 KB | One link between a fare and a route |
| frequencies.txt | 220 | 4 | 8 KB | One time window with a fixed gap between buses for a trip |
| routes.txt | 55 | 6 | 5 KB | One bus route |
| shapes.txt | 135,640 | 4 | 4,498 KB | One GPS point on the drawn path of a route |
| stop_times.txt | 2,608 | 5 | 102 KB | One scheduled stop of one trip |
| stops.txt | 2,607 | 4 | 116 KB | One bus stop with its coordinates |
| trips.txt | 220 | 5 | 6 KB | One trip: a route run in one direction on one service pattern |

**Columns per file**

- agency: `agency_id, agency_name, agency_url, agency_timezone`
- calendar: `service_id, monday, tuesday, wednesday, thursday, friday, saturday, sunday, start_date, end_date`
- calendar_dates: `service_id, date, exception_type`
- fare_attributes: `fare_id, price, currency_type, payment_method, transfers, agency_id`
- fare_rules: `fare_id, route_id`
- frequencies: `trip_id, start_time, end_time, headway_secs`
- routes: `route_id, agency_id, route_short_name, route_long_name, route_type, route_desc`
- shapes: `shape_id, shape_pt_lat, shape_pt_lon, shape_pt_sequence`
- stop_times: `trip_id, arrival_time, departure_time, stop_id, stop_sequence`
- stops: `stop_id, stop_name, stop_lat, stop_lon`
- trips: `route_id, service_id, trip_id, direction_id, shape_id`

**Note on format.** The data is relational (flat linked files), not nested JSON. For example, one `frequencies.txt` row reads `24626201,15:00:00,18:00:00,1200`.

## 3. What one record represents

The dataset as a whole describes the scheduled public bus network of Kigali: who operates it (agency), where it runs (routes, shapes, stops), when it runs (calendar, trips, stop_times, frequencies) and what it costs (fare_attributes, fare_rules). The most detailed business record is one row of `stop_times.txt`: one bus (trip) planned to be at one stop at a given time.

## 4. Personal data

The feed describes public infrastructure and schedules. None of the 11 files has a column for people's names, phone numbers, ID numbers, e-mails or home addresses. `agency_name` identifies a company, not an individual. No anonymisation was needed and none was applied.

## 6. Known limitations

- The feed was last updated at source in April 2024, and its service dates ended in February 2021, so it may not match today's services.
- The licence is not stated on the download page, so the data is used here for coursework only and attributed to Digital Transport for Africa.
- `shapes.txt` holds about 96% of all rows; most of the relational design concerns the other ten files.

## 7. Reproducing
